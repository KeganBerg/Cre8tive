Questions people ask

How soon do we get our photos?
Portrait and family galleries are usually ready within two weeks. Events take up to three. We’ll send a few sneak peeks sooner.
How many photos will we get?
It depends on the session, but you’ll get every good one, edited by hand. That’s usually 30 or more for a family session and hundreds for an event.
Do you travel?
Yes. Sessions in and around town are included, and we’re happy to travel further for a small mileage fee.
What should we wear?
Whatever you feel good in. Solid colors and soft neutrals photograph well. We’ll send a short guide once you book.
What if it rains?
We’ll keep an eye on the forecast with you and reschedule outdoor sessions at no charge.
Can we print the photos?
Yes. Every gallery comes with a print release, so you can print and share them wherever you like.
